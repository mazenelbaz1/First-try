Chicken Pizza Recipe

  • Ingredients:
  • 1. 2 cups of cooked chicken, shredded
  • 2. 1 cup of pizza sauce
  • 3. 1 cup of mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 4. 1/2 cup of sliced bell peppers
  • 5. 1/2 cup of sliced onions
  • 6. 1/2 cup of sliced mushrooms
  • 7. 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
  • 8. 1 teaspoon of garlic powder
